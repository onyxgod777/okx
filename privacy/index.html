Last updated: June 1, 2026

Information We Collect

The Alpha Secret ("we," "our," or "us") operates the okx.thealpha-secret.xyz website as an independent OKX referral partner. We collect minimal information:

  • Referral Data: When you click our referral links, OKX may share anonymous conversion data with us (no personal identifiable information).

  • Local Storage: We store a flag in your browser's localStorage to prevent the exit-intent popup from showing repeatedly. This data never leaves your device.

How We Use Information

Data is used solely to measure the effectiveness of our referral marketing and improve user experience. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties (except OKX for referral attribution via standard URL parameters).

Third-Party Services

Data Retention

Self-hosted analytics logs are retained for 90 days and then automatically deleted. LocalStorage flags persist until cleared by the user.

Your Rights

You may clear your browser's localStorage/cache at any time. For data deletion requests, contact us at the contact information below.
